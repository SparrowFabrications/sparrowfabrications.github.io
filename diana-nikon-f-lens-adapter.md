Designed by me from scratch to fit Lomography Diana F+ and Diana+ interchangeable lenses to Nikon F mount cameras, so you can get the dreamy, lo-fi Diana look on your Nikon.

### Directions for use
To use:

1. Attach the adapter to your camera as you would a Nikon lens: line up the bayonet, push it on and twist until it clicks into place.
2. Fit your Diana lens into the front of the adapter and twist it to lock.
3. Set your camera to manual (M) exposure mode, as the adapter has no electronic contacts.

### Focal Lengths on Nikon DX Cameras
Nikon DX (APS-C) cameras, such as the D7000, have a 1.5x crop factor, so each Diana lens gives a narrower field of view than its marked focal length suggests. Approximate 35mm (full frame) equivalent focal lengths:

| Diana lens | Equivalent on DX |
|---|---|
| 20mm fisheye | about 30mm |
| 38mm super-wide | about 57mm |
| 55mm wide-angle | about 82mm |
| 75mm standard | about 112mm |
| 110mm telephoto | about 165mm |
