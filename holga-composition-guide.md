## Holga 120 Composition Guide
A composition guide for Holga 120 cameras. Camera shown is not included.

Available in three sizes to suit your chosen film format and mask combination:
- 6x4.5 cm portrait
- 6x4.5 cm landscape
- 6x6 cm square

### Directions for use
Simply slide the composition guide into the hot shoe on top of the camera.

### Supported Cameras
Tested with:
- Holga 120N
- Holga 120S

Should be compatible with any Holga 120 camera with a hot shoe.
